# Affiliate guide

This guide is for affiliates: people who promote our NFC smart cards and earn a commission on sales they bring in. It explains every screen in your account, how sales are credited to you, and how you get paid.

All amounts are in Nepali rupees (NPR). All dates and times are Nepal time.

The numbers in this guide are the programme defaults. If the store changes a setting, the screens will show the new value.

---

## 1. Create your account

Want to know more first? Press **Earn with us** in the store menu (or go to `/affiliates`). That page explains how the programme works, with the store's current figures: the standard commission rate (if one is set), how long a click counts, the holding period, the payout minimum and the payout methods. It also shows who can join, common questions and the current programme terms. Press **Create an account to apply** to start, or **Apply from your account** if you are already signed in.

1. On the store, press **Log in**, then **Create an account** (or go straight to `/register`).
2. Fill in **Full name**, **Email**, **Phone (optional)**, **Password** and **Confirm password**.
   - Your password needs at least 12 characters, with upper and lower case letters and a number.
   - If you add a phone number, include the country code, for example `+9779812345678`.
3. Tick **I have read and accept the privacy notice.**
4. Press **Create account**.

### Verify your email

1. We send a link to your email address. Open it.
2. Back on the **Verify your email** screen, press **I've verified my email**.
3. No email? Check your spam folder, then press **Send the link again**.

You cannot apply to the programme until your email is verified.

### My profile

Open **My profile** in your account menu to change your own details.

- **Your details**: change your **Full name** and **Phone (optional)**, then press **Save details**. Write the phone with the country code, for example `+9779812345678`. Leave it empty to remove it.
- **Email**: you cannot change it yourself, because your email is how you get back into your account. Contact us if it must change.
- **Change password**: enter your **Current password**, then your **New password** and **Repeat new password**. The rules are the same as when you registered, and the new password must be different from the current one. You stay signed in where you are. You are signed out on every other device, and we email you "Your password was changed".

Your name and phone here belong to your store account. Your affiliate display name and the other answers from your application are changed with **Edit profile** on the **Affiliate programme** page.

## 2. Apply to the programme

1. Log in. You land on **My account**.
2. In the menu, open **Affiliate programme**.
3. Fill in the form **Apply to become an affiliate**:
   - **Public display name** (required). This is shown to customers who use your links, for example "You were referred by Sita's Tech Tips". It can be your name, brand or page name.
   - **Country code** (two letters, `NP` by default) and **City (optional)**.
   - **How will you promote our products?** Tick at least one: Website or blog, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, Viber, Telegram, Email newsletter, Offline / in person, Other.
   - **Website or social media links (optional)**. One link per line, starting with `https://`. Up to 5 links.
   - **Tell us about your audience (optional)**.
4. Read the affiliate terms in the box. Tick **I have read and accept the affiliate terms (version …)**.
5. Press **Submit application**.

If the page says "Applications open soon. Please check back later.", the store has not published its terms yet.

### What happens next

- You get an email: "We received your affiliate application".
- Our team reviews it. You get another email with the decision.
- You can come back to **Affiliate programme** at any time to see your status and a **History** of every change.
- While you wait, you can update your answers with **Edit profile**, then **Save changes**.

### Membership statuses

| Status shown | What it means |
|---|---|
| Pending review | We have your application and are reviewing it. |
| Approved | You are an affiliate. You get a referral code and can earn commission. |
| Not approved | Your application was declined. The reason is shown. You can apply again after 30 days using **Apply again**. |
| Suspended | Your membership is paused, usually while we look into something. Your links still open the store, but new sales do not earn commission. You can still see your history. |
| Deactivated | Your membership is closed. Only our staff can reactivate it. |

### Updated terms

If we change the affiliate terms, a box called **Updated affiliate terms** appears on the **Affiliate programme** page. Read them, tick **I accept the updated terms.** and press **Accept terms**.

## 3. Your referral code and links

When you are approved, you get a permanent 8-character **referral code**, for example `K7MPX4QA`. It never changes and is never given to anyone else. You can copy it with **Copy code** on the **Affiliate programme** page.

New menu items also appear: **Affiliate dashboard**, **Referral links**, **Marketing materials**, **Referral stats**, **Earnings** and **Payouts**.

### Your main link

Open **Referral links**. Under **Your main link** you will see something like:

`https://<store address>/ref/K7MPX4QA`

Press **Copy** and share it anywhere. It opens the store home page.

Customers can also type your code in the **Referral code (optional)** box at checkout.

### Tracking links

Tracking links help you see which post or page brings orders.

1. On **Referral links**, press **New tracking link**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name**: only you see this, for example "Instagram bio".
   - **Link address**: lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens, for example `insta-bio`. Your link becomes `…/ref/K7MPX4QA/insta-bio`. **This cannot be changed later**, so choose carefully.
   - **Opens product (optional)**: pick a product to send people straight to its page, or leave **Store home page**.
   - **For campaign (optional)**: shown only when there are campaigns open to you. See "Campaigns" below.
   - **Campaign tag for your own tracking (optional)** and **Source (optional)**: short labels for your own reports, for example `dashain-sale` and `instagram`. Letters, numbers, dots, hyphens and underscores only. These labels do not join you to a campaign.
3. Press **Create link**.

You can have up to 100 tracking links. Use **Pause** to stop tracking a link on its own and **Resume** to turn it back on. A paused link still opens the store home page. Visits through it still count for you, but they appear under your main link, not under the paused link's name.

### Campaigns

Our team sometimes runs a **campaign**: a promotion with set dates, often on chosen products, sometimes with a higher commission rate. When a campaign is open to you, a box called **Campaigns you can promote** appears at the top of **Referral links**. For each campaign it shows:

- the name and our description of what to promote;
- the commission: the campaign rate, or "Your normal commission rate" if the campaign has no rate of its own;
- the products it covers (or all products) and its last day;
- your campaign coupon code, if we gave you one.

To take part, press **Make a link for this campaign** (or **Make another link** if you already have one). The **New tracking link** form opens with the campaign already chosen. Give the link an address and press **Create link**. You can also choose the campaign under **For campaign (optional)** when you make any new link.

**Only campaign links and campaign coupon codes count for a campaign.** An order that comes through your main link, or where the customer types your referral code, earns your normal rate.

- The campaign rate applies only to the products the campaign covers. Other items in the same order earn your normal rate. Products that do not earn commission still earn nothing.
- The order counts for the campaign only if it is placed while the campaign is running.
- After a campaign ends, its links keep working and still credit sales to you, at your normal rate.
- Campaign rates do not appear on the **My rates** tab of **Earnings**. They are shown in the campaign box.

### Coupon codes

Some affiliates are given their own discount coupon by our team. You cannot create one yourself; ask us if you would like one. When a customer uses your coupon, the order is credited to you. Your coupons' results appear under **Coupon codes** on the **Affiliate dashboard**.

Note: some coupons are set up so that they do not earn commission. We will tell you if yours is one of them.

## 5. How sales are credited to you

When someone clicks your link, the store remembers it on that person's browser for **30 days**.

- **Last click wins.** If the customer clicks another affiliate's link after yours, the newer click gets the sale.
- **Coupons come first.** If the customer uses an affiliate coupon at checkout, the coupon's owner gets the sale, even if they clicked someone else's link. Next comes a referral code typed at checkout. A remembered click comes last.
- **One affiliate per order.** A sale is never split.
- **Your own purchases do not count.** If the buyer's account, email or phone number is yours, the order is not credited to anyone.

### What does not count

- **Orders that are not paid.** A placed order is not a sale. Commission is created only when payment is confirmed.
- **Cancelled orders.**
- **Clicks from bots**, search engines, and link previews (for example, when WhatsApp, Facebook or Viber shows a preview of your link).
- **Very fast repeat clicks** from the same internet connection (more than 30 a minute).
- **Clicks after 30 days.** The remembered click runs out.
- **Clicks or sales while you are not approved** (pending, suspended or deactivated).
- **Products or coupons that do not earn commission.** Check the **My rates** tab on **Earnings**.
- **Customers who clear their browser, switch device or use private browsing** before buying. The store cannot see your earlier click. Ask them to type your referral code at checkout.

## 6. How commission works

### How much you earn

Open **Earnings**, then the **My rates** tab. It shows your default rate and what you earn on each product. Some products may show "No commission".

Commission is worked out on what the customer paid for the product after any discount. Delivery charges are not included.

*Example (the rate here is only an example):* a customer buys a card for NPR 1,500 with a NPR 100 discount. At a 10% rate you earn 10% of NPR 1,400 = NPR 140.

### The commission journey

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | The order is paid. The commission waits for **14 days** in case of a refund. Each commission shows "available from" a date. You get an email: "You earned commission on a new order". |
| **Approved** | The 14 days have passed with no refund or hold. Normally this lasts only moments before the next step. |
| **Available** | The money is yours to request. You get an email: "Commission added to your available balance". |
| **In payout** | It is part of a payout request that has not been sent yet. |
| **Paid** | We have sent it to you. |
| **Reversed** | Taken back because the sale was refunded, or cancelled by our team. You get an email: "Commission reversed". |

The check for finished holding periods runs every hour, so a commission becomes available within about an hour of its date.

### Refunds, disputes and holds

- **Refunds.** If a customer gets a refund, the commission on the refunded part is reversed. A partial refund reverses only part of the commission. *Example:* you earned NPR 140 on an order. Half the product value is refunded, so NPR 70 is reversed and NPR 70 stays.
- **Refund after you were paid.** The reversed amount is taken from your available balance. This can make it go **below zero**. Your next commissions fill the gap first.
- **Payment disputes and chargebacks.** If a customer disputes a payment with their bank or wallet, commissions on that order that are not yet available are put **On hold**. If the store wins, the hold is lifted. If the store loses, the commission is reversed as if it were refunded.
- **Other holds.** Our team may hold a commission for review, for example if something looks unusual. A held commission does not move forward until the hold is lifted. The **Earnings** page tells you how many are on hold.
- **Manual corrections.** Occasionally our team may add or remove an amount with a written reason. It shows on your **Statement**.

## 7. Getting paid

### Add your payout details

1. Open **Payouts**.
2. Under **Payout details**, press **Add payout details** (or **Change** if you already have some).
3. Choose **Paid by**: **Bank transfer**, **eSewa** or **Khalti**.
4. Fill in:
   - **Account holder name**: exactly as it appears on the account.
   - For a bank: **Bank**, **Branch (optional)** and **Account number** (6 to 30 letters, digits or dashes).
   - For eSewa or Khalti: **Wallet mobile number**, the 10-digit number registered with the wallet (for example `98XXXXXXXX`).
5. Enter **Your password**. We ask for it because this decides where your money goes.
6. Press **Save**.

We then email you "Your payout details were changed". **If you did not make the change, reset your password and contact us straight away.**

After saving, the screen shows only a masked version, for example "Nabil Bank ••••1234". If a payout is already in progress, it still goes to the old details. The new details apply to your next request.

### Request a payout

You can request a payout when **all** of these are true:

- your membership is **Approved**;
- you have saved payout details;
- you have no other request in progress; and
- your **Available** balance is at least **NPR 1,000**.

Pending, approved and held commission does not count towards the minimum.

1. On **Payouts**, under **Request a payout**, press **Request NPR …**.
2. Check the amount and destination, then press **Request payout**.

A request always covers your **whole** available balance. You cannot choose a smaller amount. For example, if you have NPR 2,450 available, the request is for NPR 2,450.

That amount then moves from **Available** to **In payout**. That means it is set aside for this payout and cannot be used for anything else.

### Payout statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Requested | Waiting for our finance team to review. You can still press **Cancel request**. |
| Approved | Checked. The transfer will be made soon. It can no longer be cancelled by you. |
| Paid | Sent. The transfer reference is shown in **Payout history** and in your email. |
| Rejected | Declined, with a reason. The amount goes back to your available balance. |
| Failed | The transfer did not go through. The amount goes back to your available balance. Check your payout details. |
| Cancelled | You cancelled it. The amount goes back to your available balance. |

Our team makes each transfer by hand from the company bank or wallet, then records the reference. There is no fixed payout day. You request when you are ready.

### Tax

Tax treatment of affiliate payments (including any TDS withholding) is **to be confirmed by the business**. Nothing is deducted at the moment. We will update this guide and tell you before anything changes.

## 8. Your dashboard and reports

### Affiliate dashboard

Choose a **Period** at the top (Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, This month, Last month, Last 12 months, or **Custom dates**).

| Figure | What it means |
|---|---|
| Clicks | Visits through your referral links in the period. Bots and rapid repeat clicks are not counted. |
| Unique clicks | Clicks from different visitors. The same visitor is counted again after 24 hours. |
| Attributed orders | Orders credited to you and placed in the period, paid or not, excluding cancelled orders. |
| Conversion | Attributed orders per 100 unique clicks. |
| Paid orders (under Attributed orders) | Your orders whose payment was confirmed in the period. |
| Sales | What customers paid for products on your orders paid in the period, after discounts, before refunds. Delivery is excluded. |
| Commissionable sales | The part of those sales that earns commission (excluded products and non-earning coupons are left out). |
| Commission earned | Commission created on orders paid in the period. Any amount "since reversed" is shown underneath. |
| Refunded | Product value refunded in the period on your orders. |

The **Your balance** box shows your balance right now, whatever period you picked:

| Balance | What it means |
|---|---|
| Pending | Commission inside the holding period. Not yet payable. |
| Approved | Holding period passed; waiting to be released to the available balance. |
| Available | Payable now. Can go below zero when a sale is refunded after its commission was paid. |
| In payout | In a payout request that has not been paid yet. |
| Paid | Paid out to you, all time. |
| Reversed | Taken back by refunds or voids, all time. |

The dashboard also shows charts, **Products sold**, **Coupon codes** and **Referral links**. Open **How these numbers are counted** at the bottom for these definitions on screen.

### Referral stats

The **Referral stats** page (titled "Referral performance") shows the last **7**, **30** or **90 days**: clicks, orders placed (with cancelled ones counted separately), conversion, order value, results **By link**, and **Recent referred orders**. For each order you see its reference, value, date, how it was credited (Referral link, Affiliate coupon, or Referral code at checkout), the items and its status. You never see customer names or contact details.

### Earnings

- The top shows **Available**, **Pending** (this figure includes approved commission), **Paid out** and **Reversed**.
- **Commissions** tab: every commission, with filters **All**, **Pending**, **Approved**, **Available**, **Paid** and **Reversed**.
- **Statement** tab: every movement of your balance, for example "Pending → Available".
- **My rates** tab: your current rates (shown while your membership is active).

### Download your records

At the bottom of the **Affiliate dashboard**, under **Download your records**, press **CSV** or **Excel** next to:

- **My commissions**: commission on your orders paid in the period, with any refund reversal and current status.
- **My referred orders**: orders credited to you and placed in the period, including cancelled ones. No customer details are included.
- **My payouts**: payout requests made in the period, their outcome and transfer reference.
- **My statement**: every movement of your commission balance in the period.

Each download uses the period chosen at the top of the dashboard.

## 10. Leaving the programme

1. Open **Affiliate programme**.
2. Under **Close membership**, press **Close my affiliate membership**.
3. Optionally give a **Reason (optional)**.
4. Enter your password in **Confirm with your password**.
5. Press **Close membership**.

What happens:

- Your links stop earning straight away, and you receive "Your affiliate membership is closed".
- The affiliate menu items (dashboard, links, earnings, payouts) disappear from your account.
- Only our staff can reactivate your membership. Your referral code stays reserved for you.

**Before you close, request a payout of any available balance and wait until it shows Paid.** A closed member cannot request a payout, and a request still in progress cannot be completed while your membership is closed. Contact us if you have money left after closing.

A suspended membership cannot be closed by the member. Please contact us instead.

Closing your affiliate membership does not delete your store account.

## 11. Frequently asked questions

**Why didn't my sale count?**
Common reasons: the order is not paid yet, or it was cancelled; the customer clicked another affiliate's link after yours; they used someone else's coupon; more than 30 days passed since their click; they bought on a different device or browser; the buyer used your own account, email or phone; or the product does not earn commission. Ask customers to type your referral code at checkout to be safe.

**Why didn't my order earn the campaign rate?**
The order did not come through a link made for the campaign or a campaign coupon code; the product is not part of the campaign; the order was placed before the campaign started or after it ended; or our team switched the campaign off before the order was paid. Your normal rate applies instead.

**Why did my commission go down?**
A customer was refunded (fully or partly), a payment dispute was lost, or our team corrected a commission. Check the **Statement** tab on **Earnings** to see each movement.

**Why is my balance negative?**
A sale was refunded after its commission was already paid to you or requested. The amount is taken back from your available balance. Your next commissions fill the gap first, and you can request a payout again once the balance reaches NPR 1,000.

**When do I get paid?**
Each commission becomes available 14 days after the order is paid, if it was not refunded or held. Once your available balance is at least NPR 1,000, request a payout. Our finance team reviews it and sends the money by hand. You get an email with the transfer reference.

**Why is my "Request payout" button greyed out?**
The message above the button explains why: your membership is not active, a request is already in progress, you have not added payout details, or your available balance is below the minimum.

**Can I change my link address?**
No. Create a new tracking link and pause the old one.

**Is TDS deducted?**
Not at the moment. Tax treatment is to be confirmed by the business.
